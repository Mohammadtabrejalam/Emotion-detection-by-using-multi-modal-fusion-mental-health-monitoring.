---

## 2. Intended Use
* **Primary Use Case**: Computational research and longitudinal tracking of user affective signals for non-clinical wellness exploration.
* **Out-of-Scope & Prohibited Uses**: Clinical psychological diagnosis, psychiatric assessment, or automated medical triage (e.g. diagnosing clinical depression, generalized anxiety disorder, or bipolar conditions).
* **Target Users**: Affective computing researchers, ML practitioners, and individuals seeking structured emotional reflection tools.

---

## 3. Training & Evaluation Data Status
* **Current Prototype Data**: Synthetic tensors generated by `SyntheticEmotionDataset` (`backend/app/ml/train.py`) for pipeline validation.
* **Target Research Corpora (Phase 1+)**: IEMOCAP (acoustics/dialogue), MELD (multi-party dialogue transcripts and facial crops), CMU-MOSEI (multimodal sentiment/affect).
* **Data Splitting Policy**: Subject-independent partitioning (no speaker/actor overlap between train, validation, and test splits).

## 5. Limitations & Ethical Considerations
* **Non-Clinical Boundary**: MindSense is strictly a computational research tool. Emotion predictions are statistical approximations of expressive behaviors, not internal mental health diagnoses.
* **Sensory Variance**: Facial expressions and acoustic pitch vary significantly across cultures, accents, ambient lighting, and background acoustic noise.
* **Crisis Guardrails**: Regex-based crisis phrase detection alerts users and routes directly to professional crisis lines (e.g., 988 Lifeline).
